# Hire Trainer — How to Use

A simple guide for **Trainers** and **Gym Owners** to use the Hire Trainer feature.

---

## 🏋️ For Trainers

### How to Register Your Profile

1. Go to the **Home Page** → scroll to **Hire Trainers** section
2. Click **"I'm a Trainer"** → you will be taken to the Trainer Application page
3. Enter your **Email** and **Mobile Number**
4. You will receive an **OTP on your email** → enter the OTP to verify
5. Fill in your details:
   - Full Name, Address, City, State
   - Role (First Half / Second Half / Full Time)
   - Total Experience, PT Experience
   - Current Salary, Expected Salary
   - Specialization (e.g. Yoga, CrossFit, Weight Training)
   - Gender, Marital Status
   - Upload certificates (optional)
6. Click **Submit** → your profile is now live and visible to gym owners!

### How to View & Update Your Profile

1. Go to **Hire Trainers** → **"Browse & Search"** or open `/hire-trainer/search`
2. Click **Login** (top-right) → enter your registered **Mobile Number**
3. After login, switch to the **Trainers** tab
4. You will see **only your own profile** (your post)
5. Click the **✏️ Edit** button on your profile card to update any details
6. Make changes and click **Save Changes**

### How to Browse Gym Vacancies

1. Go to `/hire-trainer/search`
2. Switch to the **Vacancies** tab
3. Use the search bar or filters (City, Role, Salary, Experience) to find jobs
4. Click on any vacancy card to see full details and gym contact info

---

## 🏢 For Gym Owners

### How to Register as a Gym Owner

1. Go to the **Home Page** → scroll to **Hire Trainers** section
2. Click **"I'm a Gym Owner"** → you will be taken to the Login/Register page
3. Enter your **Mobile Number**
   - If you are **new**, you will see a registration form
   - Fill in: Name, Gym Name, Email, Gender
4. You will receive an **OTP on your email** → enter the OTP to verify
5. Done! You are now registered and logged in

### How to Post a Trainer Vacancy

1. After login, you will be redirected to the **Search** page
2. Switch to the **Vacancies** tab
3. Click the **"+ Post Vacancy"** button (top-right)
4. Fill in the vacancy details:
   - Role Type (First Half / Second Half / Full Time)
   - Experience required, PT Client Experience
   - Job Description
   - Specialization (e.g. Strength Training, Yoga)
   - Gender Preference
   - Salary Range (Min – Max)
   - Location (State, City)
   - Closing Date
5. Click **Post Vacancy** → your vacancy is now live!

### How to Edit Your Vacancy

1. Login and go to the **Vacancies** tab
2. You will see **only your own vacancies**
3. Click the **✏️ Edit** button on any vacancy card
4. Update the details and click **Save**

### How to Search for Trainers

1. Login and switch to the **Trainers** tab
2. Use the search bar to search by name, city, or specialization
3. Use filters on the left sidebar:
   - Location (city)
   - Job Type (First Half / Second Half / Full Time)
   - Experience Range
   - Salary Range
   - Gender
   - Availability (how soon can join)
4. Click on any trainer card to see their full profile

---

## 🔍 For Everyone (No Login Needed)

### Browse Trainers & Vacancies

1. Go to the **Home Page** → scroll to **Hire Trainers** section
2. Click **"Browse & Search"**
3. Use the **Trainers** tab to browse trainer profiles
4. Use the **Vacancies** tab to browse open gym positions
5. Use the **search bar** at the top — type and press **Enter** or click **Search**
6. Use **quick filter chips** (role type) below the search bar
7. Use the **sidebar filters** for detailed filtering

## 📋 Quick Reference

| I want to... | Go to |
|---|---|
| Register as a Trainer | Home Page → "I'm a Trainer" |
| Register as a Gym Owner | Home Page → "I'm a Gym Owner" |
| Post a Trainer Vacancy | Login as Gym Owner → Vacancies tab → "+ Post Vacancy" |
| Edit my Vacancy | Login as Gym Owner → Vacancies tab → ✏️ Edit |
| View/Edit my Trainer Profile | Login as Trainer → Trainers tab → ✏️ Edit |
| Search Trainers | Search page → Trainers tab |
| Search Vacancies | Search page → Vacancies tab |
| Browse without login | Home Page → "Browse & Search" |

---

## 🔐 Login & Account

- **Trainers** login using their registered **Mobile Number**
- **Gym Owners** login using their registered **Mobile Number**
- No password needed — login is verified through your registered email
- If you forgot your details, use the **"Forgot your login details?"** link on the login page — your details will be sent to your registered email
