## 1. What is GymPro?

GymPro is an **online gym management application** that helps gym owners run their gym operations smoothly from a single platform. It covers:

- **Member management** — Add members, track memberships, renewals, and payments
- **Trainer management** — Add trainers, assign them to members, settle salaries
- **Diet & Exercise plans** — Create and assign diet and workout plans to members
- **Attendance tracking** — Track daily member attendance (manual or biometric)
- **Finance** — Track expenses, income, pending payments, and salary settlements
- **Inquiries** — Manage prospective member leads and follow-ups
- **Reports** — Get a bird's-eye view of your gym's performance from the dashboard

---

## 2. Who Uses GymPro? (Roles)

| Role | Who Are They? | What Can They Do? |
|------|--------------|-------------------|
| **Gym Owner** | The person who owns/runs the gym | Full control — manage members, trainers, finances, diet/exercise plans, attendance, reports |
| **Trainer** | A fitness trainer working at the gym | View assigned PT members, check salary history, download salary slips |
| **Member** | A person who has a gym membership | View their own membership details, diet plan, exercise plan, attendance, and dashboard |

## 3. Getting Started — Login

### How to Login

1. Open the GymPro application in your browser
2. You will see the **Login Page**
3. Enter your credentials:
   - **Gym Owner** — Use your **email** and **password**
   - **Trainer** — Use your **mobile number** and **password**
   - **Member** — Use your **mobile number** and **password**
4. Click the **Login** button
5. After successful login, you will be automatically taken to your respective dashboard:
   - Gym Owner → Gym Owner Dashboard
   - Trainer → Trainer Dashboard
   - Member → Member Dashboard

> **Note:** Your login credentials are provided by the Gym Owner (for Trainers and Members) or by the Admin (for Gym Owners).

### 4.3 Adding a New Member

**Go to:** Sidebar → Members → Click **"+ Add Member"**

**Step-by-step:**

1. **Personal Information:**
   - Enter **First Name** and **Last Name** (required)
   - Enter **Email** address
   - Enter **Mobile Number** (required — this is their login ID)
   - Select **Date of Birth**
   - Select **Gender** (Male / Female / Other)
   - Enter **Address**
   - Select **Occupation** (from dropdown)
   - Select **Marital Status**
   - Select **Blood Group**
   - Enter **Emergency Contact Number**
   - Add any **Health Notes** (allergies, medical conditions)

2. **Photo & Documents:**
   - **Upload Member Photo** — Click to upload from your computer or use the **Camera** button to take a live photo
   - **Upload ID Proof** — Upload a document like Aadhaar, PAN, etc.

3. **Membership Details:**
   - Select **Course Package** (e.g., "1 Month Regular", "3 Months Regular") from dropdown
   - The **fees** will auto-fill based on the package selected
   - **Membership Start Date** and **End Date** will auto-calculate based on the package duration
   - Enter **Extra Discount** (if any) — the final fees will recalculate automatically
   - Fees breakdown:
     - Package Fees → Max Discount → After Discount → Extra Discount → **Final Fees**

4. **BMI Calculator (optional):**
   - Enter Height and Weight to calculate BMI during registration

5. Click **"Save"** to create the member

> **After saving:** The member's account is created. They can now log in using their **mobile number** and the password you set.

### 4.5 Membership Renewal

When a member's membership expires or is about to expire, you can renew it.

**How to Renew:**

1. **Go to:** Sidebar → Members
2. Find the member (search or filter by "Expiring" status)
3. Click the **"Renew"** button next to the member
4. A **Renewal Dialog** will open:
   - Select the **new Course Package**
   - The new **Start Date** and **End Date** will auto-calculate
   - Enter any **discount** amount
   - Select **Payment Mode** (Cash / Card / UPI / Online / Cheque / Other)
   - Enter the **amount paid**
   - If partial payment, the remaining balance will be tracked automatically
5. Click **"Renew"** to confirm

> **Renewal Types:** Standard renewal, Early renewal (before expiry), Late renewal (after expiry), Upgrade (longer package), Downgrade (shorter package).

### 4.6 Balance Payment (Partial Payment)

If a member hasn't paid the full fees, you can record partial payments over time.

**How to Record a Balance Payment:**

1. **Go to:** Sidebar → Members
2. Find the member
3. Click the **"Payment"** button
4. A **Balance Payment Dialog** will open:
   - Select **Payment For**: Regular Membership or PT Membership
   - Enter **Payment Date**
   - Enter **Amount Paid**
   - Select **Payment Mode** (Cash / Card / UPI / Bank Transfer / Cheque / Net Banking / Other)
   - **Receipt Number** is auto-generated (e.g., RCP-001, RCP-002)
5. Click **"Save"** to record the payment

> The system tracks all partial payments and calculates the remaining balance automatically.

### 4.7 Personal Training (PT) Membership

PT Membership is an **add-on** on top of a regular membership. A member can have both a regular membership and a PT membership.

#### Adding PT Membership

1. **Go to:** Sidebar → Members → Find the member
2. Click **"Add PT"** button
3. Fill in the PT details:
   - Select **PT Course Package** (e.g., "1 Month PT", "3 Months PT")
   - Select **Trainer** to assign
   - PT **Start Date** and **End Date** auto-calculate
   - Enter **fees** and any **discount**
   - Select **Payment Mode** and enter the **amount paid**
   - Add **Goals** (e.g., "Weight loss", "Muscle building")
   - Add any **Notes**
4. Click **"Save"**

#### Editing PT Membership
- Click **"Edit PT"** next to the member
- Update any details (trainer, dates, fees, goals)
- Click **"Save"**

#### Pausing PT Membership
- Click **"Pause PT"** to temporarily freeze the PT membership
- The remaining sessions and days are preserved

#### Removing PT Membership
- Click **"Remove PT"** to completely remove the PT add-on
- The regular membership remains active

### 4.15 Trainer Salary Settlement

**Go to:** Available from the Trainers or Salary section

At the end of each month, settle trainer salaries based on their attendance and incentives.

#### Settling a Trainer's Salary

1. Go to **Salary Settlements**
2. Click **"+ New Settlement"**
3. Select the **Trainer** from dropdown
4. Select the **Salary Month** (e.g., "2026-03")
5. The system will auto-fill:
   - **Monthly Salary** (from trainer profile)
   - **Total Days in Month**
   - **Present Days** (from attendance data)
   - **Absent Days**
6. Enter **Discount Days** (days not counted as absent, e.g., approved leaves)
7. **Payable Days** = Present Days + Discount Days
8. **Calculated Salary** = (Monthly Salary ÷ Total Days) × Payable Days
9. Add **Incentive** (if any):
   - Incentive Type: PT Training / Protein Sales / Member Referral / Others
   - Incentive Amount (e.g., ₹2,000)
10. **Final Payable Amount** = Calculated Salary + Incentive Amount
11. Select **Payment Mode**
12. Click **"Save"**

**Example:**

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Monthly Salary | ₹20,000 |
| Total Days in Month | 31 |
| Present Days | 26 |
| Absent Days | 5 |
| Discount Days | 2 (approved leave) |
| Payable Days | 28 |
| Calculated Salary | ₹18,064 |
| Incentive (PT) | ₹3,000 |
| **Final Payable** | **₹21,064** |

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### General

**Q: How do I get my login credentials?**
A: Gym Owners receive credentials from the GymPro Admin. Trainers and Members receive credentials from their Gym Owner when they are added to the system.

**Q: I forgot my password. What do I do?**
A: Contact your Gym Owner (for Trainers and Members) who can reset your password. Gym Owners should contact the GymPro Admin.

**Q: Can I use this on my mobile phone?**
A: Yes! GymPro is fully responsive and works on mobile phones, tablets, and desktop computers.

**Q: How do I logout?**
A: Click on your name/avatar in the top-right corner and select "Logout".

---

### For Gym Owners

**Q: How do I start using GymPro for my gym?**
A: Follow this order:
1. **Set up Master Data** — Create Course Packages, Expense Groups, Designations
2. **Add Trainers** — Add your gym trainers
3. **Add Members** — Start adding members with their memberships
4. **Create Diet & Exercise Plans** — Create templates and assign to members
5. **Track Attendance** — Set up biometric devices or use manual entry
6. **Manage Finances** — Record expenses, settle salaries

**Q: What is the difference between Regular and PT membership?**
A: **Regular** is the standard gym membership (access to gym equipment). **PT (Personal Training)** is an add-on where a trainer is personally assigned to train the member. A member can have both.

**Q: Can I give discounts to members?**
A: Yes! Each Course Package has a maximum discount limit. When adding or renewing a member, you can apply discounts up to the maximum limit. You can also give an extra discount on top.

**Q: What if a member pays in installments?**
A: Use the **Balance Payment** feature. Record each payment as it comes in. The system tracks the remaining balance automatically.

**Q: How does the salary settlement work?**
A: At month-end, go to Salary Settlements, select the trainer and month. The system calculates salary based on attendance (present days vs. total days). You can add incentives for PT training, protein sales, member referrals, etc.

**Q: Can I export data?**
A: Yes! You can export the member list to Excel/CSV from the Members page.

---

### For Trainers

**Q: Can I see all gym members?**
A: No. You can only see the members that are assigned to you for Personal Training (PT).

**Q: How do I check my salary?**
A: Go to Salary Settlements from the sidebar. You can filter by date range and download your salary slip as a PDF.

**Q: Can I assign diet plans to my members?**
A: Diet plans are assigned by the Gym Owner. You can view the diet plans assigned to your PT members.

---

### For Members

**Q: Can I change my diet or exercise plan?**
A: No. Diet and exercise plans are assigned by your Gym Owner or Trainer. Contact them if you need changes.

**Q: How do I check if my membership is expiring?**
A: Your Dashboard shows your membership expiration date and a countdown. The Membership page has all the details.

**Q: How do I mark my attendance?**
A: If your gym has biometric devices set up, you can check-in using your fingerprint when you arrive and check-out when you leave. If not, the Gym Owner records your attendance manually.

**Q: Can I see my payment history?**
A: Yes! Go to Membership page to see all payments you've made, including dates, amounts, and receipt numbers.

---
